## Deploying Your Hugo Portfolio Site on GitHub Pages

Instructions for this can be found in the [GitHub Pages Deployment Guide](https://gohugo.io/hosting-and-deployment/hosting-on-github/).

Make sure to follow the steps to push your generated site to the `main` branch of your GitHub repository. This will make your portfolio site live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<your-repo-name>/`.

Now your portfolio is live and accessible to the world! And every time you update your content, just regenerate the site and push the changes to GitHub to keep your portfolio up to date. That's a CI/CD pipeline in action!
